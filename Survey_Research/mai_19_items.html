MAI 19-item subset

George M. Harrison — March 2025

Important caveat: This subset was identified through iterative psychometric modeling to achieve adequate model-data fit within the sample used in the study. It has not been independently validated as a stand-alone instrument, and the study does not establish that it supports valid inferences and uses in other contexts. Researchers considering using these items should consult the original article and exercise appropriate caution, particularly for longitudinal measurement (where only partial scalar invariance was supported). If you use these stems to develop a survey instrument, I recommend consulting established survey-research guidelines, such as Dillman, Smyth, & Christian (2014).

Each item uses a fully labeled 5-point response scale:

  • Not at all typical of me
  • Not very typical of me
  • Somewhat typical of me
  • Fairly typical of me
  • Very typical of me
